Challenges of digital preservation 

	Reliance on digital systems means that technical failures, software 	obsolescence, or data corruption can lead to the loss of valuable 	information. To combat this libraries should implement a robust digital 	preservation framework, including regular data migration, cloud based 	storage solutions, and redundant backup systems.

	Another concern is potential loss of quality in digital conversion. While 	high-resolution scanning can improve preservation accuracy, some aspects 	of the authenticity of physical objects cannot be replicated digitally.

	Security risks are another key issue in digital preservation. Digital 	collections are vulnerable to cyber threats, including hackings, 	unauthorized access, and data breaches.
